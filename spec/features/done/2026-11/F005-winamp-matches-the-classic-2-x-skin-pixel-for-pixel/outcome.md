# Outcome

Winamp's main window, equalizer and playlist are now our own pixel art at the exact Winamp 2.x layout, with our own 5x6, 9x13 and playlist pixel fonts; the owner approved the look on the live site. Merged in PRs #138 (pipeline, fonts, diff harness), #139 (main window), #140 (equalizer) and #141 (playlist, last merge `dc0bb7f`). The diff harness reports 63 of 63 layout rects at the reference geometry; the pixel share (95–100% differing at tolerance 0) is reported, not gated, by owner decision. No original Winamp bitmap, `.wsz` file or reference pixel is in the repository; the survey worker read a Webamp base-skin clone in `/tmp` before the own-art decision reached it and deleted it. Follow-ups: the volume slider drops the EQ preamp boost (F006), and the shell overlay blocks three title-bar buttons (F007).
